Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Review our clear, uncompromising commitment to user privacy and browser-isolated data processing.

1. Introduction

Welcome to GST Invoice Pro ("we", "our", or "the website"). We are dedicated to upholding the highest standards of data integrity and digital privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of information handled when you visit our website, how that data is stored or processed, and your rights concerning that information.

2. Information You Provide

Invoice Generation Information

When utilizing our GST Invoice Generator, you enter commercial information such as business names, addresses, Goods and Services Tax Identification Numbers (GSTIN), client names, item descriptions, rates, discounts, and banking coordinates.

Zero-Server Architecture: All invoice calculations, tax breakdowns, number-to-words conversions, and preview renders happen exclusively on the client-side within your browser. Your invoice details are NEVER transmitted to, processed by, or stored upon our web servers or any third-party database.

Contact Information

If you contact us via email or submit a message through our support channels, we collect the correspondence details you voluntarily supply (such as name, email address, and query content) solely to address your specific inquiry.

3. Automatically Collected Information & Server Logs

Standard static web hosting providers (such as Cloudflare, Netlify, Vercel, or GitHub Pages) automatically log basic technical parameters necessary to transmit web pages, including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser version, operating system, and timestamp of file requests. These logs are maintained purely for infrastructure reliability and cybersecurity defense.

4. Local Storage and Cookies

GST Invoice Pro does NOT use persistent tracking cookies to follow your activity across the internet. Instead, we utilize browser localStorage solely to improve your direct user experience:

  • gst_invoice_pro_theme: Remembers your preference between Light Mode and Dark Mode.
  • gst_invoice_pro_draft_v1: If you explicitly click "Save Draft", your current invoice inputs are saved inside your device's browser sandbox for future restoration.

You have complete control over this data and can purge it at any time by clearing your browser cache or clicking "Clear Draft" in the application.
