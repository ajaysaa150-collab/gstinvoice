WHO WE ARE & OUR VALUES

About GST Invoice Pro

A mission-driven initiative delivering zero-friction, browser-native GST invoicing tools to Indian entrepreneurs and independent professionals.

Who We Are

GST Invoice Pro is an independent digital tool purpose-built for Indian entrepreneurs, sole proprietors, independent contractors, creative agencies, and small enterprise teams. We believe issuing a compliant Goods and Services Tax invoice shouldn't demand hefty software subscriptions, complex cloud databases, or cumbersome accounting certifications.

Our Mission

Our mission is straightforward: democratize professional commercial billing for every micro-business in India. We strive to eliminate administrative overhead by offering a fast, clean, and completely client-side utility that outputs audit-ready tax documents on demand.

Why We Built This Tool

Traditional ERP packages and cloud accounting services often impose:

  • Mandatory account registrations and marketing emails.
  • Rigid multi-tier subscription models that penalize early-stage businesses.
  • Bloated dashboards filled with irrelevant enterprise bookkeeping modules.
  • External server storage of sensitive client price agreements and commercial data.

Recognizing these pain points, we created GST Invoice Pro to execute 100% inside your browser. No remote database is contacted, no sensitive customer data leaves your device, and no recurring fees are requested.

What We Offer

Our platform brings together everything needed for everyday statutory billing:

  • Dual Tax Mode Support: Accurate automatic division between Central & State GST (CGST + SGST) for intra-state sales, and Integrated GST (IGST) for interstate transactions.
  • Standard Slab Presets: Seamless switching across 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28% GST brackets.
  • Real-time Document Mirroring: A responsive A4 invoice canvas that reflects pricing discounts and item additions instantaneously.
  • Indian Rupee Formatting & Number-to-Words Engine: Automatic conversion of invoice totals into official Indian currency word denominations (Thousands, Lakhs, Crores, and Paise).
  • Local Browser Persistence: Private draft saving using HTML5 localStorage so your work is never lost across tab sessions.
  • Zero-Watermark PDF Printing: Print directly or save pristine PDF files utilizing modern browser print styling.

Our Approach: Simple, Transparent, and Secure

We believe software should respect user privacy by design. Because GST Invoice Pro has no backend and relies on vanilla web standards, your commercial transactions remain entirely between you and your clients.

Who Can Use GST Invoice Pro?

Our generator is tailored to satisfy the billing requirements of diverse professionals across India:

  • Freelancers & Independent Consultants: Tech developers, UI/UX designers, copywriters, and legal advisers billing clients nationwide.
  • Small & Medium Businesses: Trading firms, wholesale suppliers, and regional distributors.
  • Retailers & Shop Owners: Local merchants issuing bills for goods with applicable tax breakdowns.
  • Agencies & Service Providers: Marketing studios, event organizers, and architectural firms.
  • Contractors & Technicians: Independent tradespeople providing commercial services.
